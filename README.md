<h1 align="center">SacriFall</h1>

<img width="1600" height="900" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d68ab644-3707-467c-a0be-a9425b640392" />

<p>Daven is a participant in Haven Delhi. One week ago he left his home for the event but got lost on the way there. Thankfully the event ended up getting delayed...

BUT DAVEN'S STILL STUCK IN THE FOREST! Can you help him find his way out of the wood in time to reach the Game Jam? Jump around and strategically use his quilts to create platforms and make a path to the exit!

SacriFall is a puzzle platformer where you control Daven, purposefully lose his Quilts to make a path, and <strong>bounce back</strong> straight towards the exit. Whenever you hit an obstacle, you use up a _Quilt_ and create a platform, with the  caveat being Daven get knocked back to the start.

But be careful! Lose to many of Daven's Quilts and he'll collapse on the spot and miss Haven!
</p>
